# Kasanagi: The Seven Paths — adapted canon

Kasanagi is the owner's original universe. Before it became a Moodicons crew, the parts that followed existing series
too closely were changed. Keep these changes in every future drawing, product page and story text.

| Original idea | Too close to | Kasanagi canon now |
|---|---|---|
| Red spiral emblem, "Path Arts" | A well-known ninja series' clan crest | **Sun emblem** (eight rays) for Path Arts |
| "Breathing Arts", "Sakura Breathing" | A well-known demon-hunter series' breathing styles | **Pulse Arts**; Saliha's style is **Sampaguita Pulse** |
| "Devil Gifts"; Bantug's stretching body | A well-known pirate series' powers and hero | **Anito Gifts**; Bantug's power is **Wind-Step** (speed, gusts, impact) |
| "Aura (hunter-style energy)" | A well-known hunter series' energy system | **Diwa** (spirit and intention sense) |
| Dumalag: silver hair and forehead headband | A well-known ninja series' teacher | Slate-blue hair under a navy hood, eye pendant |
| Saliha: pink floral kimono and cherry blossoms | Demon-hunter series swordswomen | Maroon wrap with gold collar, sampaguita in her hair |

The Seven Paths: Path Arts, Pulse Arts, Diwa, Anito Gifts, Clan Techniques, Relics, Resonance.
